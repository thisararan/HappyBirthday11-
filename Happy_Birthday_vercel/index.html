FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser, with no account or payment.

Where are my photos stored?

Only on your device. They are packed into the link or file you share, never uploaded to a server.

Can they open it on a phone?

Yes. Every layout is fully responsive and works in any modern browser.

How do I send it?

Copy the shareable link, or download the standalone HTML file and send it on any app.
